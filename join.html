We recruit from physics, engineering and computer science. What matters is wanting to build hardware that learns.

PhD students

Projects span nanofabrication, magnetic and optical measurement, device modelling and learning algorithms. Most students do some of each. Funded places are advertised through the Department of Physics; we also support applications for external scholarships.

Postdocs

We advertise funded positions when they open. We are also happy to support fellowship applications from strong candidates whose interests overlap with ours.

Collaborators

We work with academic and industrial partners on unconventional computing, nanomagnetism, magnonics, photonics and hardware learning. Get in touch if you see a fit.

Undergraduate and MSc projects

Imperial students can take projects with the group through the department's project allocation.
